# TruckStudio

**Version:** v0.1.3-alpha

⚠️ **Important Note:** This tool is exclusively designed and developed for **Euro Truck Simulator 2 (ETS2)**. It does not support American Truck Simulator (ATS) or other games.

## Overview / What does this program do?
**TruckStudio** is a powerful and easy-to-use desktop application designed to modify and enhance your save games in **Euro Truck Simulator 2**. 

Instead of manually editing complex code or struggling with the game's console, TruckStudio provides a modern, clean graphical interface where you can instantly tweak your player profile, manage your truck fleet, and manipulate the game world. Whether you want to quickly add money to your account, magically repair your cargo after a crash, unlock all cities, or give yourself infinite fuel, TruckStudio safely reads your game save, applies the cheats or modifications you select, and saves it back instantly so you can continue playing without interruptions.

## Enabled Features
The following features are fully implemented and functional in version v0.1.3-alpha:

### 👤 Player Profile
- **Edit Money:** Instantly change your account balance (€).
- **Edit Experience:** Modify your total Experience Points (XP) to level up.

### 🚛 Trucks & Trailers
- **Fix Fleet:** Instantly repair all your owned trucks and trailers to 100% condition.
- **Refuel Fleet:** Refuel all your trucks to 100% capacity.
- **Teleport (Camera 0):** Quickly trigger teleportation. *(Note: Requires `g_console` and `g_developer` set to `1` in your ETS2 `config.cfg` file).*

### 💼 Jobs Market
- **Fix Cargo Damage:** Accidentally damaged your delivery? Magically reset active cargo damage back to 0% to ensure full payment upon delivery.
- **Freight Market Cargo Injection:** Inject custom cargo offers directly into any company. Select any source/destination cities and companies, choose your cargo, and TruckStudio will automatically calculate a compatible trailer and update the game's economy event queue safely.

### 🌍 World & Map
- **Unlock All Garages:** Instantly purchase and unlock every garage across the entire map.
- **Discover All Dealerships:** Reveal all truck dealerships instantly.

### 🔧 Pro Tuning & Cheats
- **Max Skills:** Max out all your driver skills (ADR, Long Distance, High Value, etc.) with a single click.
- **Infinite Fuel (50,000 km):** Extend your fuel tank capacity massively so you rarely have to refuel.
- **Restore Fuel System:** Revert your fuel tanks back to normal vanilla capacity.

## Disclaimer
This is an Alpha release. Always make sure to back up your save files before making any modifications.
